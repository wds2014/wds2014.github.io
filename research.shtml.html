Research Interests

My research focuses on reliable AI systems that can model complex and uncertain data. I am broadly interested in probabilistic machine learning, generative models, and multimodal representation learning.

  • Probabilistic machine learning & Bayesian analysis: Variational inference, hierarchical models, matrix factorization, and uncertainty-aware modeling.
  • Generative models: Diffusion-based models, auto-regressive models, and efficient adaptation for downstream tasks.
  • Multimodal representation learning: Vision-language models, multimodal large language models, and domain alignment.
